Help on every website

One extension. Four gentle helpers.

Simple navigation

Hides clutter and pop-ups. Shows one clear step at a time, so you always know where to go next.

Read aloud

Tap any text and hear it spoken. Hard words are explained in short, plain language.

Easy form filling

Forms are split into small questions with hints. Saved details fill in for you, and you check before sending.

Money practice

Practise paying, sending and saving in a safe sandbox. No real money. No worry.
