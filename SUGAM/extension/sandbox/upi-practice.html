🌟 The 3 Golden Rules
  • 1. Never enter a PIN to receive money: A UPI PIN is only used to SEND money from your bank.
  • 2. Always check the name and amount: Look at the screen before typing your PIN.
  • 3. Stop. Check. Ask: If someone rushes or threatens you, hang up and call 1930.
