National Disability Pension Scheme 2026

Published on October 15, 2026 | Health and Welfare Department

The Ministry of Social Justice and Empowerment has announced the updated monthly pension for eligible citizens under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act. Starting November 1, 2026, beneficiaries will receive ₹1,500 directly in their Aadhaar-linked bank accounts.

Important Date: Applications open on October 25, 2026. The application fee is ₹0.

How to Apply

Applicants must submit their disability certificate, Aadhaar card, and bank passbook copy. You can submit the form online at your local Seva Kendra.
